Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Bag is an iOS app for shopping lists, recipes and an AI assistant for cooking and planning lists ("Bag AI", formerly "Chef AI"). This policy explains which personal data the app processes, why, who receives it, and what rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Controller

Bag is developed and operated by crystovh (independent developer).
Contact for all privacy matters: betreffen_skalar.2n@icloud.com

2. What Data We Process, Why, and on Which Legal Basis

2.1 Account

To use the app you need an account. We process your email address, your password (stored only as a hash) and the display name you choose. The display name is shown to people you share a shopping list with.

Purpose: creating and securing your account, password reset, showing you to list members.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract). Providing an email address is required to create an account.

Each account also has a usage class (e.g. “normal”) that determines how many Bag AI requests you can make per hour and per day (see section 2.4).

2.2 Shopping lists

Your shopping lists are stored on our servers so they can be synced and shared. This includes list names, categories, items, quantities, status and notes, the members and roles of each list, and invitations you create. Items record which member created or changed them.

If you share a list, other members of that list can see its content and your display name.

Purpose: providing the shopping list feature, syncing between devices and list members in real time.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

2.3 Recipe library

Your recipe library is stored in your account so it is available again after reinstalling the app. This includes saved and planned recipes (name, ingredients, steps, cooking time, servings, calories, dietary tags, image link), favorites and ratings and your recipe categories. Your recipe library is visible only to you; it is not shared with members of your shopping lists.

Changes you make while offline are kept on your device and sent to our servers later. Before a sync removes recipes from your device, the app keeps one backup copy of your library on your iPhone only; each new backup replaces the previous one.

Purpose: providing the recipe library and keeping it across reinstalls.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

2.4 Bag AI

When you use Bag AI, the messages you type, any photos you attach, and links to recipe pages you send are transmitted to our server and forwarded to an AI language model to generate an answer. Our server also runs an automated safety check on each request (text and images) and blocks requests that violate our usage policy.

Depending on your question, the AI may perform a web search for recipes. The search terms are generated from your message and sent to a search provider (see section 3). Recipe URLs you send are fetched by our server.

Chat history: your text messages and the AI's replies are stored in your account so you can continue conversations later. Photos are not stored; they are only used to answer the message they were attached to. If a request is blocked by the safety check, we store the blocked message together with the category and reason for blocking.

Bag AI preferences: if you set your own rules for Bag AI in the app settings (e.g. “always vegetarian” or “no nuts”), the active rules are sent with each Bag AI message to our server and the AI language model, and are included in the safety check. They are stored only on your device and are not stored on our servers.

Lists in Bag AI: Bag AI can help you plan lists (e.g. a packing list) and suggest changes to your existing lists. With each Bag AI message, the app sends the names of the lists you are a member of (and your role in them) to our server and the AI language model. The content of a list (categories and items with name, quantity, note and checked state) is only sent for lists you have allowed in that chat (at most five); you can withdraw this at any time. This list information is not stored by us and is not part of the safety check. What Bag AI suggests (a new list or changes) is stored as part of its reply in your chat history and, like all tool calls, appears briefly in our server logs. Nothing is written to your lists until you confirm it in the app. Which lists you allowed in a chat is stored only on your device.

Sync to Bag: when you add a recipe's missing ingredients to your shopping list, the ingredient names and amounts and the names of your shopping-list categories are sent to our server and the AI language model, which assigns each ingredient to one of your categories. Nothing of this is stored; you can review and change every assignment before saving.

Quick add in the Bag: when you type items into the field at the top of your shopping list (e.g. “4 apples, 500 g flour and milk”), the text you typed and the names of your shopping-list categories are sent to our server and the AI language model, which splits the text into separate items with amounts and assigns each one to a category. Nothing of this is stored; you can review and change every item before saving.

Backup AI providers: if our primary AI provider (Groq) is unavailable or has reached its capacity limit, the same request — with the same data and nothing more — is sent through the AI router Requesty (EU endpoint) to a backup model hosted in the EU (Nebius, Google Cloud Vertex AI or Microsoft Azure, see section 3). We only use backup models that, according to the provider, do not store the data or use it for training.

Usage limits: to keep the service available for everyone and to prevent misuse, our server counts your Bag AI requests (chat messages, recipe imports, Sync to Bag, quick add) per account. The count is kept only in the server's memory for at most 24 hours and is not stored in a database. When you reach a limit, the app shows a notice.

Purpose: providing the Bag AI assistant (including planning lists), your conversation history and the category suggestions for Sync to Bag and quick add; preventing misuse of the AI service.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for the assistant and history; Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in preventing misuse and keeping the service available) for the safety check, backup providers and usage limits.

2.5 Technical data

When the app communicates with our servers, technical data such as your IP address, request time, and app/device information sent by iOS is processed and may appear in short-lived server logs.

Purpose: delivering the service, security, troubleshooting.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure and functioning service).

2.6 Data that stays on your device

Your app settings (e.g. language) and the shopping-mode Live Activity are stored only on your iPhone and are not sent to our servers. Your Bag AI preferences are also stored only on your iPhone; they are sent with each Bag AI message as described in section 2.4, but not stored by us. Deleting the app removes this data.

Camera access is only used when you choose to take a photo for Bag AI. The app contains no advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs.

4. Retention

  • Account and shopping lists — until you delete them or your account.
  • Recipe library — until you delete recipes or categories in the app, or your account.
  • Chat history — until you delete individual conversations or all conversations in the app, or your account.
  • Photos sent to Bag AI — not stored; processed only for that request.
  • Usage counts for Bag AI limits — only in the server's memory, for at most 24 hours.
  • Server logs — kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting, then deleted.

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, profile, usage class, chat history, recipe library, list memberships and all shopping lists you own, including for members you shared them with. Items you added to other people's lists remain in those lists but are no longer linked to you. All app data on your device is removed as well. If you cannot access the app, you can also request deletion by email at the address in section 1.

5. AI-Generated Content

Answers from Bag AI are generated automatically and can be wrong or incomplete. This applies in particular to recipes, quantities, cooking times, lists, allergens and food safety. Please check every answer before you use it. You are responsible for how you use the answers. Lists created or changed with Bag AI are only suggestions and may be missing items. We provide Bag AI without any guarantee that its answers are correct, complete or suitable for your purpose; this does not limit any liability that cannot be excluded by law.

6. Automated Decisions

The safety check described in section 2.4 automatically blocks requests that violate our usage policy. It only affects whether Bag AI answers a message and has no legal or similarly significant effect on you. We do not carry out profiling.

7. Security

  • All connections between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Authentication tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • Database access is restricted by row-level security so that users can only access their own data (including their recipe library) and lists shared with them.

8. Children

Bag is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly process data of children under 16.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows the current version.
